Source: [reviewlib/capacity_check.py at the inspected head](https://github.com/alex-mextner/review-cli/blob/6be25c8a54b8a7091fc4e0eb0e58ac38e1c80bdd/reviewlib/capacity_check.py), blob `e9761d006feec6bd374e44e4c447e5f13d07a102`.

The local path records how this probe ran, not a durable artifact download promise.

Reproduction setup and assertions:

# Case A: no proactive exhaustion evidence and no cooldown entries.

assert board_capacity_report()['available'] == ['fixture:one', 'fixture:two']

The inspected functions have no cooldown-cause predicate, underlying sample freshness check or actual account binding. These are contract-level findings; the probe does not prove that the exact fixture text is produced by a real upstream caller. Task #472 requires repository regression coverage and #705 requires real producer/consumer validation before acceptance.
